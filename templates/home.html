About Research & Extension (MIS)

The Research & Extension (MIS) is designed to streamline academic workflows at J.H. Cerilles State College - Dumingag Campus.

Our platform enables efficient management of research proposals, tracks extension activities, and provides comprehensive analytics to support data-driven decision making.
